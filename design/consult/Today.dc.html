What the software already knew
  • The child weighs 7 kg — it is on the vitals chart it just rendered.
  • The allergy list is on the patient record, two inches above the form.
  • J06.9 is the ICD-10 for the syndrome the doctor is about to name.
  • The four-drug URI regimen is in the clinical bundle, with doses.
What the redesign changes
  • The form becomes a spine of four decisions, one visible at a time.
  • Every value the system knows is offered as a tap, never a blank.
  • Typing stays available for everything — it stops being the default.
