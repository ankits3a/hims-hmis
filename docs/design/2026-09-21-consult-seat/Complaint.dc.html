Why chips and not a dropdown

A dropdown takes the caret and gives it back somewhere else. A doctor mid-sentence loses their place. Chips sit under the field, take one tap, and leave the cursor alone.

Why both scripts

The complaint is heard in Hindi and written in English. The chip carries both, so the doctor taps the words the patient said and the record keeps the words the chart needs.
