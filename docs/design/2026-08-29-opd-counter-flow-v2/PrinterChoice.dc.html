Which printer for which piece of paper

The receipt is fighting you because of its format, not its content. A 4 × 6 label is die-cut — fixed height, take it or leave it. An 80 mm roll (72 mm printable) is continuous: it is as long as the job needs. That single difference is why Next Steps has to be sacrificed on one and fits comfortably on the other.

The short answer — three printers, not one
80 mm THERMAL
Token slip · OPD payment receipt
The same printer and the same roll for both. One machine at the counter, one paper stock, one spare — and no reason to buy a label printer for the billing desk at all.
24-PIN DOT MATRIX
Pharmacy · lab · radiology invoices
Two-part carbon stationery. Your instinct was right — this is what pharmacy shops use, and the reason is the carbon copy and the fact that it does not fade.
A4 LASER
Prescription · reports · IPD final bill
Anything that runs to pages, carries a logo, or leaves the building for an insurer or a TPA.

Not buying, and what would change that
4 × 6 is off the list entirely. Two other tempting purchases are off it too — for now.
4 × 6 LABEL PRINTER
DEFERRED
Sample tubes and film jackets will need barcode labels one day. There is no lab module yet, so there is nothing to label. Buy it the week the lab goes live, not before — and buy it for the collection desk, never for billing.
SELF-SERVICE BILLING KIOSK
DEFERRED
Medanta Patna runs them. They pay off where most patients are repeat, urban and carrying a smartphone. A first-time walk-in from a village who cannot read the screen turns a kiosk into a queue with an extra step in it — revisit once repeat visits are the majority, and measure that before spending.
THIRD CARBON PART
ASK THE CA
Two-part covers the patient and the day-book. A green accounts copy is only worth the extra cost per set if your CA actually wants paper.

One machine at the billing counter, not two. At roughly 300 OPD receipts a day the 80 mm roll costs about ₹36, against roughly ₹450 a day in 4 × 6 labels — before the second printer, its spares and a separate consumable to keep in stock.
Two things to confirm before you order. Prices are indicative ranges for planning, not quotes — ask two local vendors and check what ribbon and stationery actually cost in Hajipur, because consumable supply matters more than the machine. And put the GST treatment past your CA: consultation as an exempt healthcare service versus pharmacy as a taxable supply of goods is what decides whether a document is a receipt or a tax invoice — and that, not the printer, is what sets the retention rule.
