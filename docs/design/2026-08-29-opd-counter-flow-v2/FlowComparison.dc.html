Why Flow 3 probably suits you
Two counter crossings instead of four. One paper instead of two. No unpaid slip to chase and no receipt that has to explain it is not a token. At around 300 OPD a day you may not need three separate front counters at all.
What it costs you
One clerk now holds registration, doctor choice and cash. That is a real separation-of-duties loss — the drawer and the queue are in the same pair of hands. It needs a blind cash count and a daily concession report to stay honest.
