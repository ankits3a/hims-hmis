WHY IMPACT, HERE AND NOWHERE ELSE
Carbon, not a second print. One pass gives the patient and the day-book a copy. Thermal would mean printing twice and hoping both match.
It does not fade. Thermal goes blank in a drawer in a Bihar summer. A GST invoice has to be legible for years, not months.
Any number of lines. One item or thirty — the form just runs on. A die-cut label cannot do that.
Ribbon, not rolls. One ribbon runs for millions of characters and survives dust, heat and bad mains.
Slow and loud. ~15 s and about 60 dB for this bill. Fine at a pharmacy hatch, wrong at an OPD counter with a queue.
No usable barcode. Impact bars scan badly. Print the invoice number in plain digits and let the counter type it.
No Hindi at all. Code pages only, and graphics mode is unusably slow. This form stays English.
