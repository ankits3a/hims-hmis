Identity and the patient

the family phone, the same name, no UHID
1One mobile number for the whole family; three patients on it this monthDELIVERYThe WhatsApp PDF is locked with year of birth + UHID last four, so a brother cannot open a sister's report. A mobile that matches two people at the counter asks for the name. Add: “this phone belongs to” at registration so the message greets the right person.CHANGE
2Three Sunita Devis, all 32, on the same morningALLNo seat ever searches by name after reception: the token, the UHID and the S barcode carry identity. The bench scans; it never picks from a list. Names are shown to confirm, never to select.HOLDS
3Walk-in with an outside prescription and no UHIDRECEPTIONReception cannot order without a patient. The registration counter already registers in four fields without leaving the seat (RC-4). Add the same four-field register-in-place to lab reception, so Sanjay does not send them across the hall.CHANGE
4The ordering doctor is outside the hospitalRECEPTION · DELIVERYThe order needs a clinician today (requiresClinician). Add “referred by” as a named outside doctor; the report goes to the patient only, and the doctor's-screen column reads “outside”.CHANGE
5Two records for one patient; last month's result sits under the other UHIDVERIFYDelta checks and “previous” already follow the merge chain to the canonical patient, so the comparison finds the other record once merged. Duplicate detection is the registration counter's job.HOLDS
6A relative comes to collect; the patient is bedridden at homeDELIVERYThe counter records who collected — name and relation — on every print or in-person hand-over; the code requires it. In-person-only tests (HIV) are the exception: the patient, with ID.HOLDS

Money

the unpaid slip, the scheme card, the outside test
7Ayushman / PM-JAY, TPA or corporate patient with dues on paperDELIVERYNever held. tpa · pmjay · corporate payers and day-care encounters are exempt from the print interlock in code. The report prints like any paid one.HOLDS
8Slip paid for three tests; the prescription has fourRECEPTIONReception shows billed / not billed per line. The tube is drawn either way; only the unbilled line of the report waits for money. Sanjay can bill it there or send them back to the front desk.HOLDS
9Test not done here — vitamin D, HLA-B27, culture sensitivity to a referral labRECEPTION · BENCHThe sent_out state exists and is driven by nobody: no partner lab, no dispatch manifest, no returned-report import. Reception cannot even tell the patient a day. Plan 17-M.GAP
10Health camp or corporate screening: 200 samples in a morningRECEPTION · COLLECTIONNothing for bulk: no pre-printed barcode sheets, no camp roster import, no batch registration. The chains do this daily. Plan it as its own slice; the ELISA plate map on the instruments board is the bench half of it.GAP

The sample

tea before the fasting test, the smudged label, the neonate
11“Bas ek chai” — not fasting for the glucoseCOLLECTIONVikas has the button: Not fasting → send back with a morning slot; the other tubes are still drawn. If the patient insists, the draw is marked non-fasting and the report says so beside the value.HOLDS
12Label smudged in the ice box; the bench scanner cannot read itBENCHTyping an S number by hand is allowed, and re-labelling is a witnessed act. Add: “re-label · second person” on the bench, recorded like the absurd-value override, so a mislabel cannot be made silently.CHANGE
13Ward tube arrives with a handwritten name and no barcodeBENCHRejected as unlabelled, charged to collection; a free recollection lands on the ward. The bench never accepts a name in place of a barcode.HOLDS
14Three-day-old baby: heel prick, half a millilitreCOLLECTION · BENCHReference ranges resolve by age in days at collection (0–28 d, 28–365 d …). Insufficient volume is a named rejection; a paediatric tube is flagged in the queue so Vikas plans the draw.HOLDS
15A man's tube shows a pregnancy hormone: tubes swapped at the chairBENCH · VERIFYDelta checks catch a value that jumps against the same patient's history; nothing catches a value impossible for the sex or age on the record. Add an impossibility rule at entry that parks the result and flags both tubes drawn in that minute.CHANGE
16Patient never returns for the recollectionRECEPTIONA daily 07:00 sweep cancels anything recollection_pending for 7 days and raises the credit note in the same transaction. Nothing is left hanging on a worklist.HOLDS

The result

a critical value at 9 pm, a machine down, a correction after WhatsApp
17Potassium 6.8 at 21:10; OPD over, ordering doctor's phone offBENCHThe call opens itself and every attempt is logged; only a read-back closes it. Add the ladder: ordering doctor → duty medical officer / casualty → patient or attendant directly, each a named contact with a time limit, so a technician is never left dialling one number.CHANGE
18Doctor wants the numbers by phone before the pathologist signsVERIFYThe single-operator path releases a provisional report, marked as such, when no pathologist is on. Separation of duties blocks the same person entering and verifying. Add: the doctor's screen may show unverified values with that word on them.CHANGE
19Chemistry analyser down; reagent for HbA1c not deliveredBENCH · DELIVERYThe analyser statuses (qc_locked · calibration_due · interface_down · maintenance) are declared and written by nobody; no reagent stock; and nothing tells the patient “your report will be tomorrow 10:00”. Plan 17-E plus one delay message.GAP
20Server or internet down for an hourALLLabels have a downtime_kit path with pre-printed serials mapped at accession; the instrument bridge queues locally. Add a visible downtime mode on collection and bench so the paper register and the later reconciliation are one screen, not a habit.CHANGE
21Haemolysed sample gives a false high potassiumBENCHhaemolysed is a named rejection with attribution; the value is never entered, the tube is recollected free. The instrument's own haemolysis index, where it sends one, should park the result before a human sees a number.HOLDS
22Report already on WhatsApp; a value was wrongVERIFY · DELIVERYA correction is a new version (amended, reason coded); the old PDF cannot be recalled, so v2 goes out on the same channel saying what it supersedes, and the doctor's screen shows the retraction. Critical corrections are telephoned again.HOLDS
23Night shift: one technician draws, runs, enters and wants to releaseBENCH · VERIFYSeparation of duties is enforced (sod_violation_blocked); the single-operator release is allowed only as a provisional report and is counted for NABL. The design shows both.HOLDS

Law and language

consent, notification, Hindi
24HIV screen reactive; malaria positiveRECEPTION · DELIVERYHIV needs recorded consent before the draw and is in-person only; reactive screens are repeated and go to ICTC counselling. Malaria, dengue, hepatitis feed the IDSP register by event. The counselling referral is the in-person hand-over itself: the counsellor, not the counter, gives the result.HOLDS
25Patient reads Hindi onlyDELIVERYThe SMS and WhatsApp text are already bilingual in the app; the report body is English by NABL convention. Add Hindi headings and the flag words (उच्च / निम्न) on the patient copy; the doctor's copy stays as is.CHANGE
26Ultrasound-style sex disclosure asked of the labALLNo lab orderable may report foetal sex — pinned false by a database check the catalogue cannot route around. PCPNDT is radiology's law; the lab is simply unable to break it.HOLDS
