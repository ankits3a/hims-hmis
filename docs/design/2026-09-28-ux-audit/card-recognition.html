4 · What changes and why

  • Dead end after "Recognise". The card now leads somewhere: step 2 picks one of the holder's visits today and the pinned act "Take to the bill" opens the billing counter on that visit with any coupon code already filled in. A card linked to the patient's UHID is honoured by the bill on its own (the server resolves members by patient); the counter only has to carry the visit and the coupon.
  • An expired card looked like an active one. Status is now the first thing on the card: green "Active", brick red "Expired 14-Sep-2026", "Suspended", "Cancelled". A card that cannot be used has no apply act anywhere, its benefits read "cannot be used", and the one next act is "Bill at full rate".
  • Card code split mid-code at 390. Every card and coupon number is one unbreakable Plex Mono run (no wrap); at phone width it gets smaller, never split.
  • 896px centred column wasted ~60% at 1440. Three columns, as on every station: the card in hand on the left, the numbered flow in the centre, the day's cards on the right.
  • Nobody could see who the card belongs to. The lane shows the holder's name, age, sex and UHID, and whether the card is linked to this patient; an unlinked card is sent to Reconcile rather than applied.
  • "Benefits" was a bare list. Each benefit shows how many visits are left (3 of 4) — counts, never rupees, because no counter screen shows a sales figure (E-32).
  • An unknown card was a dead end too. "No match" now offers grace honour, which the server already has, behind a supervisor's approval.
  • Raw dates and states. Dates print as 31-Mar-2027 and states as words; the server's disclosure sentence stays word for word, as step 4.

5 · Questions for the owner

  • Cash-value benefits. Some plans carry a rupee balance rather than a count of visits. Showing "₹1,250.00 left" would be a money figure on a counter screen, which E-32 rules out. Board default: show only "balance left — see the bill". Keep that, or allow the balance?
  • Expired card, member wants to renew now. Card sales are switched off (ruling O-15 open), so the board says "ask the card's issuer". Should the counter instead honour a card within a few days of expiry (a grace period), or never?
