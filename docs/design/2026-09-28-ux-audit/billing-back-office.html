5 · What changes and why

  • Five filter-style tabs → one ranked list. Refunds, Recon, Day book, GSTR-1 and Unbilled visits were tabs you had to visit to find out whether anything was waiting. “Needs you today” pulls every waiting thing into one list, most urgent first, with a chip (PAY · APPROVE · RECON · UNBILLED · DAY BOOK · GSTR-1) saying where it came from.
  • The pages move into the header menu (Refunds, Receipts, Reconciliation, Day book, GSTR-1, Unbilled visits) — no sidebar, no tabs. Each page is one unfiltered list; the forms (request a refund, void a receipt, upload a statement) become entries in the menu rather than sitting on top of the work.
  • At 390 the vouchers to pay were below two forms. On the phone the list is the page and the vouchers sit at the top of it; opening one stacks its steps and pins “Pay” at the thumb.
  • Black-and-neutral styling → Paper & Pine. Same tokens, header, source chips, left lane and pinned act as the pharmacy office board the owner approved, so both back offices read as one product.
  • Unbilled visits get a date and a way out. Each row shows the visit number, patient, doctor ID and the visit’s own date and time; opening one offers “Raise the missing bill” (new) instead of a dead-end list.
  • A voucher is paid as a numbered flow: requested → approved → issued → who takes the money → which drawer → print. The ID number is typed once and never shown back (as today); the approver’s flags (“consultation already seen”, “line already delivered”) stay visible and never block.
  • Money reads as money. ₹ with two decimals, dates as 28-Sep-2026, printed numbers (RFV/…, RCP/…, INV/…) and UHIDs — never paise, internal ids or status words. Builds on PR #358’s patient picker, receipt lookup and names on vouchers.
  • No credit on this screen. Credit is the owner’s alone (ruling 28-Sep-2026); the billing office sees refunds and settlements, never a “give credit” act.
