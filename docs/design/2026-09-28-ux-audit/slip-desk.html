4 · What changes and why

  • No page padding (content flush at x=0) → the house three-column station: 296 px lane, centre with 24 px padding, 352 px list. Nothing touches the window edge.
  • Focus ring overlapping the intro line → the scan box is 48 px tall with its own label above and 8–14 px gaps; the ring is a 3 px outer glow with room around it.
  • Single column, ~80% of 1440 empty → the matched patient lives in the left lane (the read-back never scrolls away), the photo and its choices in the centre, today's slips on the right.
  • Unstyled "Choose File / No file chosen" → a house secondary button "Choose a photo" beside Capture; the native input is hidden behind it (on a phone it still opens the rear camera).
  • Steps appear and disappear → one numbered flow (Scan · Check · Photograph · File) with a pinned dock whose Enter does the single next act; R retakes, Esc clears a wrong match.
  • No sense of the day → one unfiltered list, waiting slips first by age, then retakes, then filed; "Clocks running" collapsed below. No filter tabs.
  • Kind hidden in a dropdown → three radio cards with a one-line meaning each; the default stays "Prescription from this visit".
  • Silent second filing → the lane shows what is already on file for this visit and says "this adds page 2" (derivable today from the patient's documents list).

5 · Questions for the owner

  • Is a missing slip a clock? The list and "Clocks running" treat a finished consultation with no photographed slip as waiting. Should it escalate (e.g. to the OPD supervisor after 30 minutes, or MRD at day end), or only show?
  • Lost or torn slip: may the desk file against a visit found by name/UHID when the QR cannot be read, or must the doctor's room reprint the slip first?
