6 · Questions for the owner

Q1 · Aadhaar as evidence. The reason chips invite "Aadhaar shown, not stored". Should a merge ever record the last four digits of the Aadhaar seen as proof, or never? (Aadhaar Act §29 limits storing it; the board assumes never.)
Q2 · Records with a sealed (confidential) flag. Should merging a sealed record need the MS to break the glass first, or is the MS's approval itself enough? The board does not yet draw this.
