What changes and why

  • Candidates showed only name, UHID and “match 0.87”. Each patient now sits beside the card, field by field — name, age and date of birth, sex, masked mobile, district, last visit — each marked agrees, differs or not on the card, with a count (“4 of 4”). A permanent link is decided by comparing people.
  • A raw score was the only strength signal. Strength is a word — Strong, Possible, Weak — worked out from the agreeing fields, not from the name alone. DECIDED: this replaces the code’s “show the number, not a band” rule; the band cannot pre-select anything, and the fields sit beside it.
  • Weak and strong matches had the same black “Link this patient” button, placed raggedly. Choosing is a radio at the top of each column; nothing is chosen for you. One green act sits in a pinned bar and names what it will do: “Link card AH-FAM-018733 to Suresh Kumar Yadav · UHID 0048213”.
  • A weak link cost the same one click. Choosing a Weak patient spells out what differs, asks how the clerk knows (ID seen / called the mobile / partner corrected) and asks them to confirm the link carries their name. The reason is saved with the link (the resolve call already takes a note).
  • “Dismiss” sat under Link with no reason asked up front. It is now “None of these…”, a secondary button apart from Link, opening a short list of reasons, and it says what happens to the card afterwards.
  • Lapsed restores showed a raw key (“consult-visits”) and no date. They join the one queue with a LAPSED RESTORE chip, and in hand show the benefit in words, when it was given back, when the card ended, and the bill number.
  • The page was one long stacked column. It becomes the house station: header menu, card in hand on the left, one numbered flow in the centre, one unfiltered queue on the right with NEW CARD / CAP OVER / LAPSED RESTORE chips, clocks folded below.

Questions for the owner

  • A benefit given back on a card that has ended — does the member keep it? Today the system gives the free visit back and only raises a flag. Choices: (a) honour it until the card is renewed, (b) cancel it quietly, (c) honour it only if renewed within 30 days. This is money, so it is yours.
  • Should partners be asked to send date of birth and sex in the holder file? Without them the card side of the comparison has only the name and mobile, and most matches can only be Possible. It changes the partner agreement’s file format.
