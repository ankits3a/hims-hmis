## Owner rulings on the boards' questions (28-Sep-2026)

1. The billing manager may accept a bank short-settlement as a bank charge, up to ₹50.00 per receipt. Anything above that goes to the owner.
2. Refunds above ₹25,000.00 are approved by the owner.
3. A membership card's rupee balance is never shown at the counter. It shows on the bill only.
4. An expired card is never honoured; the visit is billed at full rate.
5. A benefit given back to a card that has ended can be used only once the card is renewed.
6. Card partners are asked to send date of birth and sex in their holder files. This is a request, not a condition of the contract.
7. Aadhaar is never stored as evidence, not even the last four digits.
8. Merging a sealed (confidential) record needs the Medical Superintendent to record a break-glass first.

## Decided by the standard

- A missing slip after a consultation shows in the slip desk's list and clocks, and goes to MRD at day end.
