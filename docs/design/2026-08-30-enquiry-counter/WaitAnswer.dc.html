“How long will I have to wait?”

कितनी देर लगेगी?

The most-asked question at this desk, and the one the current console answers worst — it prints “about 55 minutes” in the waiting colour, as though it were measured. Nothing in the system computes it. There is no consult-duration history, no estimator, no ETA anywhere in apps/core. This sheet is what she can honestly say today, and what one small piece of work would let her say instead.

Median of the last 10 4m 45s × 12 ahead = 57 min
Median, not mean — one 40-minute consultation would drag a mean into uselessness, and long consultations are exactly what a busy OPD produces. Per SESSION and not per doctor-forever: the same consultant is a different speed at 9 a.m. and at 4 p.m.
NEVER A POINT
about 55 minutes
A number to the minute is heard as a promise. At 12:05 she is wrong and it is her fault, standing in front of the person she was wrong to. This is how an estimate becomes a grievance.
A RANGE, ROUNDED UP
an hour to an hour and a half
Median to 90th percentile, rounded outward to the nearest half hour, and never below the position count. Being early is free; being late is the complaint.

Lead with the position. The time is the second sentence.
A position is a fact she can defend; a duration is a forecast she cannot. And a person who knows they are twelfth will check the board themselves instead of coming back to ask her.

“It's busy enough today that your turn may not come. Take tomorrow morning, or Dr Maan is free in Room 106 now.” The screen says this before she does — the alternative is offered, not hunted for.
WHAT IT WOULD TAKE and what has to be true before any of it is said out loud
1 · One reader over opd_queue_entries: median and p90 of done_at − called_at for the open session, last ten rows.
4 · Suppressed below four samples, and suppressed entirely when the session is paused or the doctor is off the floor.
2 · No new table, no new column, no migration. The rows are already being written on every consultation.
5 · Rounded outward to the half hour before it reaches a human. The precise number never leaves the server.
3 · The same reader feeds the waiting-hall board, so the board and the desk cannot disagree in front of the patient.
6 · Never spoken to a caller. They will arrive ninety minutes later and hold the hospital to a number from before lunch.
DECIDED: until that reader exists, the console shows the four measured numbers and no duration at all. A blank where the estimate would go is a smaller failure than a confident wrong number, because the patient can hear the difference between “twelve ahead of you” and “fifty-five minutes” — and only one of them can be broken.
