The edge-case pass

run before the console was drawn, not after

Thirty-four ways the seat goes wrong, sorted into the ones that moved the layout, the ones that are a rule the screen enforces, the ones that are ordinary policy, and the ones this design honestly does not solve. Six changed the console, and one of them changed it more than the whole question inventory did.

1
The six that changed the drawing
Everything else on the console follows from the question inventory. These came from asking what happens when the day is not ordinary.
Acidity and a heart attack say the same sentence
edge 18 · the biggest one
पेट में जलन with pain going down the left arm is not a stomach. A symptom router that answers with a department is a router that sends an infarct to a General Medicine queue fourteen deep. So the three red-flag questions sit ABOVE the department name, not below it, and they are phrased as things she asks rather than things she decides. Any yes, or any doubt, goes to Emergency. She is never placed where “it is probably nothing” is available to her.
The right rail stopped changing
edges 1, 2, 21, 22, 32, 33
The first draft varied the right column with the query. With eight people at the counter and no token to order them, a rail that moves is a rail she has to re-read. It is now fixed standing instruments — who is waiting, what is held, what she can do, the two panic keys — and every shape-specific thing lives on the left. Predictability beats density when the interval between questions is forty seconds.
“Away” became a first-class state
edges 3, 31
One seat, twenty-four hours. She walks somebody to Emergency; she eats; she goes to the toilet. The line has to go somewhere and the counter has to say something. A button that rolls the hotline to Counter 1 and puts a sign on the desk costs one control and closes the most frequent unhandled minute of the day.
Every live answer carries when it was true
edges 13, 15
The doctor left twenty minutes ago and nobody closed the session; the roster says he is in. She tells four people he is sitting and all four are angry with her, not with the roster. confirmed 11:04 · 2 min ago is not decoration — it is the difference between a fact and a guess wearing a fact's clothes, and it lets her soften the sentence herself when the stamp is old.
The wall has to work with the power off
edges 12, 34
Forty per cent of the day is answers that never change, so on the worst day of the year they are the only thing that still works — if they are not trapped behind a login. That makes the wall a laminated card on the desk as well as a strip on the screen, and it constrains what may go on it: ten items, no live numbers, nothing that goes stale between reprints.
Night is a replacement, not a dimming
edges 27–30
At 02:41 nothing about queues, symptoms or departments is true, and a white 1440-pixel screen is the brightest object in a dark lobby. The night console keeps four things and drops the rest — and half its wall exists to say what is shut and what stands in for it. It uses the dark tokens already in styles.css, so this is a mode the design system anticipated rather than a new palette.

THE ONE HOLE THE PASS FOUND — RULED BY THE OWNER, 30-AUG-2026
The IPD counter closes at 20:00. This seat runs to 08:00. Every “is my brother admitted”, every visitor pass, every attender arriving off a late bus from Chhapra between eight at night and eight in the morning has nowhere to be sent — and that is exactly the window in which people arrive frightened, having been telephoned, or off the last train.
RULED: the ward desk absorbs it after 20:00. So the enquiry seat never sends anybody to a dark counter after eight — it sends them up, with a ward name and a sister's name, and the wall says so in the same cell that carries the daytime answer. The ward sister now owns identity checks and pass issuance overnight, and that is a real load she should be told she is carrying.
Three ways out, and they cost different things
1 · CHOSEN. The ward desk owns it after 20:00, formally — free, and the ward carries it
2 · This seat gets the pass book at night — one more thing on a person already alone
3 · The IPD counter runs to 22:00 and the gap shrinks to the quiet hours — costs a shift
Option 1 was chosen. It costs nothing and it is almost certainly what already happens — the value of the ruling is that it is now written down, so the ward can staff for it and the console can say it out loud instead of leaving Ekta to improvise at 21:30.
