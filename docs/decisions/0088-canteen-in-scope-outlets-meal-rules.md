# 0088 — The canteen comes into HMIS

- **Date:** 2026-10-26   **Status:** Ruled. This replaces the "canteen out of HMIS scope" line in brainstorm 19
  (27 Aug 2026). Decision 0058 (no money on patient diet) stands.
- **Area:** canteen (#977 CN-1, #981 CN-2), diet, IPD bill, payroll, GST

## Questions put to the owner, and the rulings

1. **Scope?** The whole canteen comes into HMIS now.
2. **Who runs it?** Both. Some outlets are in-house, and some are run by a caterer who pays the hospital rent.
3. **Attendant meals?** All three ways: paid at the counter or by UPI, charged to the IPD bill, or included by bed class.
4. **Staff meals?** It depends on the staff category. Meals are free for some, subsidised for some and full price for
   others. Some categories have a salary deduction, and some get a free night-duty meal. Finance sets these as rows in a
   table, and nothing is hard-coded.
5. **Families who cannot pay?** They get free or subsidised meals, approved by the finance department.
6. **FSSAI licence?** The hospital holds it.

## GST positions (found on public sources at the owner's request; the CA confirms)

- Food given to in-patients on medical advice is part of exempt healthcare. Keep patient diet in-house: a 2026 tribunal
  reading taxes in-patient food supplied by an outside contractor.
- Canteen food to attendants, visitors and staff is taxed at 5% without input tax credit.
- Rent from a caterer is renting of immovable property, taxed at 18%.
- Canteen recovery from permanent staff under their contract is generally not a supply (CBIC Circular 172/04/2022).
  Recovery from contract or deputed staff may be taxed, and advance rulings differ. Finance sets the treatment per
  category.
