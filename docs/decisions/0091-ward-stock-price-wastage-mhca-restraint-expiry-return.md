# 0091 — Ward stock prices, wastage, expiry returns and the MHCA restraint rules

- **Date:** 2026-10-27   **Status:** Ruled. The owner accepted every default of the WM-2 spec ("specs yes").
- **Area:** IPD wards (#983 WM-2). It extends decision 0089 (when ward stock is charged) with how much and by whom.

## Rulings

1. **Price of a ward-stock charge.** A medicine is charged at the pharmacy batch price used for ward issues (#787). A
   consumable is charged at the hospital tariff and never above its MRP. An item inside a package is not charged
   separately.
2. **Opened and wasted.** Charged at full price, with a reason. The ward in-charge may waive the charge, and the waiver
   is logged with who and why. Wastage caused by staff error is never charged.
3. **Mental Healthcare Act 2017.** On a psychiatric ward a restraint follows s.97: a psychiatrist orders it, the
   nominated representative is told within 24 hours, and the register produces the monthly report for the Mental Health
   Review Board.
4. **Expiring stock returned from the ward.** Lots expiring within 30 days go back to stores at no cost to the ward's
   cost centre. Stores handles the vendor return or the write-off.

## Also ruled with the spec (DECIDED, standard practice)

- An item found expired on the shelf is never charged to a patient.
- A fall or pressure injury present on admission does not count against the ward; indicators follow the NABH key
  performance indicators.
