# 0086 — Housekeeping: in-house and contractor staff in one module

- **Date:** 2026-10-26   **Status:** Ruled.
- **Area:** housekeeping (#973, #974), contractors, stores

## Questions put to the owner, and the rulings

1. **In-house or contractor?** Mostly in-house, with some staff outsourced. One module handles both. Each attendant is
   marked in-house or with a contractor's name, and both do the same tasks with the same proof.
2. **Staffing level?** Not fixed. Attendants are added as the load grows. Screens show the load per person (beds and
   tasks each) instead of a gap against a target.
3. **Contractor penalties?** None for now. A contractor's missed rounds and late cleans appear in reports only, and no
   deductions are made on their bill.
4. **Chemicals and consumables?** They are issued from the master inventory/store module to each floor and tracked
   monthly per floor.
5. **Patient requests?** A patient may raise a housekeeping request from the door QR without logging in. Only four
   request types are offered (clean, toilet, spill, bin), and no patient data is collected.
