# 0087 — Laundry: in-house plant and per-piece vendor

- **Date:** 2026-10-26   **Status:** Ruled.
- **Area:** laundry and linen (#978 LD-1, #979 LD-2), vendor, stores, compliance

## Questions put to the owner, and the rulings

1. **In-house plant, vendor, or both?** Both.
2. **How is the vendor priced?** Per piece. The dock counts every item out and back, and records the weight as well.
3. **Pieces the vendor loses or damages?** These are reported every month. Their cost is recovered from the vendor's
   bill only when the vendor's record that month is below par: more than 0.5% of pieces lost or damaged, or more than
   10% of batches late. The system proposes the recovery at each piece's replacement cost, and the owner or finance
   approves it.
4. **Tagging?** Bundles are counted by hand for now. The owner is buying barcode or RFID tags per piece soon, so the
   data carries a piece identity from day one.
5. **Staff uniforms?** The hospital washes uniforms for nurses, lab technicians, OT staff and housekeeping.
6. **Licences for the in-house plant?** The hospital holds the wastewater consent and the boiler licence. Their
   expiry dates go on the compliance calendar.
