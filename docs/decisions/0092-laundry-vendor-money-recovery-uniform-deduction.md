# 0092 — Laundry vendor money, recovery and uniform deduction

- **Date:** 2026-10-27   **Status:** Ruled (owner: "specs yes", all proposed defaults accepted).
- **Area:** laundry and linen (#979 LD-2), vendor payables, stores, HR exit. Extends decision 0087.
- Number taken with room left for parallel lanes (0088–0091); renumber at merge if needed.

## Questions put to the owner, and the rulings

1. **Replacement cost of a lost or damaged piece?** The last purchase price of that item from a stores goods receipt;
   if the item was never received through stores, the price on the item master.
2. **Paying the vendor's bill?** The payable is the pieces accepted at the dock that month times the rate card in force
   on the return date, plus GST. A bill above that is held until stores approves the difference.
3. **Uniforms not returned on exit?** They block the no-dues clearance. The cost is recovered from the final settlement
   at replacement cost less 1/12 for each month of use (nothing after a year), with HR approval. The appointment letter
   must say so (a deduction under the Payment of Wages Act).
4. **A small month?** If fewer than 1,000 pieces went to the vendor in the month, the vendor is below par on loss when
   more than 5 pieces were lost or damaged, instead of more than 0.5%, so one piece cannot swing the test. The
   late-batch test (more than 10% of batches) is unchanged.
