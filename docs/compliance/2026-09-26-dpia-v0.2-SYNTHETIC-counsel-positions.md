## §3: Is a Class-0 payload outside cross-border transfer?

**Working position: yes, with one condition.** Aggregates are not personal data, but a small count can
identify a person ("1 HIV-reactive result today"). So:
- every Class-0 figure below **5** is sent as `<5`;
- no Class-0 payload carries a free-text field.

**Build against it:** a small-cell suppression step in the fact-sheet builder, tested.

## §3: The router that picks a provider per request

**Working position: agreed.** The router is usable only with a pinned model and provider, a signed DPA naming
that sub-processor, and Class 0 only. Otherwise it is not used.

**Build against it:** the provider pin is configuration, and the code refuses to start inference with an
unpinned router.

## §3-A: Speech-to-text carve-out (cloud ASR, not region-pinnable)

**Working position: NOT accepted.** `VOICE_SEARCH_ENABLED` stays `false` in production until in-region or
on-premises speech-to-text exists, or a real ruling accepts the carve-out.

**Build against it:** nothing new. The flag and the per-use audit already exist.

## §4: Lawful basis for Class-1 (de-identified) clinical context

**Working position:**
- The treating purpose, under the notice and consent taken at registration, covers de-identified drafting
  support for the treating clinician.
- It does **not** cover training, benchmarking or any use outside the patient's own care.
- An HIV or other sensitive record never enters a Class-1 payload, even de-identified, because its rarity
  re-identifies.

**Build against it:**
- the registration notice text carries one sentence on AI drafting support, with a human always signing;
- sensitive-flagged records are excluded at the payload builder, and a test asserts it.

## §4: Staff notice for engagement and calibration data

**Working position:** a staff notice is required before either mechanism records anything. Working text:

> *The system records how you use AI drafts (whether you accept, change or reject them, and how long you spend
> reviewing) to keep the AI safe and to decide how much it may do on its own. It is never used to assess your
> individual performance, pay or discipline. You can see your own record.*

**Build against it:**
- the notice shows once per user and its acknowledgement is recorded;
- the self-view exists before the data is collected.

## §6: Erasure against medical-record retention and audit immutability

**Working position:**
- Clinical records and their audit trail are retained under the medical-record retention obligation, and
  erasure requests against them are declined with that reason recorded.
- Personal data **outside** the clinical record (marketing consent, secondary contact numbers, wallet and
  loyalty data, patient-app preferences) is erasable.
- That erasure is built as crypto-shredding before the Significant Data Fiduciary self-assessment window
  (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027).

**Build against it:** per-subject keys for the non-clinical fields first, and an erasure register that records
each decline and its reason.

## Laboratory copilot (Plan 17-F): scope note

The lab stations' copilot drafts (reflex suggestion, report comment, IDSP L-form, run summary, bench move)
**ship deterministic**: rules and data, no inference request. So they are outside this DPIA's inference scope.
Any of them later moved to a model is Class 1 at most, excludes sensitive tests, and triggers a DPIA revision
before it runs.
