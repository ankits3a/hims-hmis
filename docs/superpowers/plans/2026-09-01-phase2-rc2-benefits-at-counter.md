## 1. Why this phase

RC-1 built the rails; RC-2 makes the money on them correct before RC-3 renders it. The owner's design demands that picking a returning member **reprices the bill column before billing is ever opened** — a benefit that only appears at invoice time is a benefit the clerk cannot quote — and the owner's **BEST SINGLE BENEFIT** ruling (2026-08-31) demands the seat show *which chip won and why the others lost*.

Recon (this session, inline) **refuted the phase's own premise, which is the cheapest thing a spike can do**: the counter quote already runs the entire Plan 09 benefits contest. `feeQuote` → `previewInvoice` → `priceDraftWithBenefits`, and `PricedLine` already carries `candidates[]` **and** `winner`. There is no membership wiring to build and no winner/loser wire type to design. What is actually missing is narrower, sharper, and mostly about **refusal**: coupons cannot physically reach the quote, referral proposes nothing, member benefits do not yet stop at the self-pay share, and one existing writer would make a naive referral discount pay money out on visits nobody referred.

This is the sixth phase running in which the honest answer was *arm and gate*, not *build*. **No deploy anywhere in this series** — production has never left `commissioning`; code-complete + green evidence is the finish line.

## 2. Spike — answered by reading, before authoring

- **S1 — does the counter quote path already compose member benefits?** **YES, fully.** `feeQuote` (`charge-rules.ts:64`) calls `previewInvoice`, which is one line: `priceDraftWithBenefits(db, input, now).priced` (`invoices.ts:594`). `PreviewInvoiceInput` already declares `patientId` **and `couponCodes`** with a comment saying exactly why ("a counter that quotes high and bills low teaches its clerks not to trust the quote"). Membership needs **zero** integration work. **The one real gap: `feeQuote(db, encounterId, now)` accepts no codes and forwards none, and `feeQuoteRoute` is `@Param("encounterId")` only — so a coupon presented at the counter can reach the invoice but never the quote.** That asymmetry is T1.

The losers and *why* they lost already travel. RC-2 designs no new type for this; it owes only that the field survives the quote and is proven non-empty by execution.

## 3. Design decisions

- **D1 — Membership at the counter is ARMED, NOT BUILT, and no money-path line is written for it.** S1 settles it. RC-2 adds no membership call, no new source, no quote plumbing for the percentage benefit. What RC-2 owes membership is *proof by execution with the flag on* that the quote and the invoice that follows it agree to the paise, plus the refusal gates below. **DECIDED:** RC-2 does not flip `MEMBER_BENEFITS_ENABLED` in any deployed environment — the flag is armed *in tests*, and the `.env` flip is a commissioning act the owner holds, like the deploy. Catalogs are empty by Plan 09 DD3 and the synthetic book lives out-of-git at `/opt/hmis-context/`.
- **D2 — Coupons reach the quote through the same door as the invoice.** `feeQuote` gains `couponCodes?: string[]` and forwards it into the `previewInvoice` input it already builds; `feeQuoteRoute` takes them as a repeatable query parameter, zod-parsed at the boundary. **The boundary is declared, not inherited** — RC-1 T1 was born from `receiptBlockSchema` silently stripping a field the web was already sending, and a quote route that accepts codes it does not declare would resurrect that exact class one screen over.
- **D3 — A referral proposes from a RESOLVED ATTRIBUTION, never from the `referralSource` enum.** S3's trap is the whole argument. The new `AdjustmentSource` reads the encounter's attribution link (`attribution_ids` / `partner_ref_map`, resolved once before pricing exactly as `composeBenefits` resolves instruments — `propose` stays pure and synchronous, `tariff` stays byte-untouched). No counterparty resolved ⇒ **no candidate, not a zero candidate.** `external_rmp` never proposes under any resolution: it is a *capture value*, its payout is unlawful (IMC 2002 cl. 6.4), and the source refuses it explicitly with its own reason string rather than by falling through. Source order becomes `[rule, manual, membership, coupon, referral]` — referral last, because RC-1's tie-break ruling ("a standing hospital rule beats a commercial instrument; a paid durable membership beats a one-shot") extends to "a benefit the patient bought beats one a channel partner brought", which is the version explainable across a counter.
- **D4 — The partner LEDGER entry is the attribution link, NOT a commission accrual.** The design asks for "a partner ledger entry for month-end commission"; `COMMISSION_ACCRUAL_ENABLED` stays **OFF** (O-8 unruled, and the brainstorm's O-1 default keeps every gated lane structurally off). RC-2 records *which counterparty this discount was proposed under* on the priced line's candidate `reason` and the existing attribution row — the fact travels, the money does not move. **DECIDED:** no accrual is written, no `commission.accrued` event is emitted, and no `partners.*` permission leaves `NOT_YET_MODELLED`. The month-end lane is Plan 21's; RC-2 must not open a second home for it.
- **D5 — Enrol and apply are two permissions from day one, and enrol is new.** *Apply* is the shipped `membership.instrument.recognise` (front_office holds it — correct, the clerk applies). *Enrol* becomes `membership.instrument.enrol`, declared on the membership manifest and granted to `front_office_supervisor` and `membership_admin`, **never to `front_office`** — the owner's ruling that this is "a ruling, not a UX nicety". **DECIDED:** `membership.catalog.manage` STAYS in `NOT_YET_MODELLED` and its reason is amended to name this phase; RC-2 builds no catalog screen (that is Plan 22 T1) and will not mint a key to a door that still does not exist — the same argument `seed-roles.ts` already makes for it. Enrolment's *route* is deliberately minimal: the permission and its refusal exist and are proven; the enrolment UI is RC-3's.
- **D6 — Member benefits stop at the self-pay share (brainstorm O-3's default, made executable).** When an encounter's `intendedPayer` is not `self`, the membership, coupon and referral sources **do not propose** — TPA/corporate/scheme rates are tariff substitution, not contestants, and a member discount stacked on a panel rate is money given away twice on a bill the hospital does not collect. The refusal is recorded as a rejected candidate with its reason, so the seat can say *why* the chip greyed out instead of silently omitting it. This is the phase's second money path and it gets fail-first treatment.
- **D7 — Corporate v0 and package v0 are naming, not machinery.** Corporate v0 = the already-recorded `intendedPayer` surfaced on the quote beside the shipped credit-extended exit, so the screen can say "bill to panel — nothing to collect" in RC-3; **no panel table, no e-authorisation record** (Plan 21 owns those; a second home is worse than a missing one). Package v0 rides the shipped `membership_plans.kind = 'package'` plus the existing `entitlement_counters`/`entitlement_movements` — a covered consult is an entitlement consumption the contest already knows how to narrow (`narrowToUsableEntitlements` drops exhausted counters rather than refusing at commit, so a member past their four free consults is simply billed). **No new table and no migration.**
