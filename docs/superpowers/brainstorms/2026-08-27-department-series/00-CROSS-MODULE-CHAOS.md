# 00 — Cross-Module Chaos — ten whole-hospital days no single department owns

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Status:** Brainstorm v1 — synthesis over documents 01–22; nothing here is ruled. Each of the 22 documents wrote its own §6 chaos walkthroughs *inside* its walls. These ten are the days where the walls are the problem: the failure crosses six or more modules, the humans who must coordinate sit in different reporting lines, and the agents must know when to go quiet. Plan numbers are the **reconciled** ones (`00-INDEX-AND-SYNTHESIS.md` §3). Row citations are `doc row-id` as printed in that document's §5 (e.g., `14 C9` = document 14, row C9).

**Conventions used in every scenario.** *System* = what the modules do without a human. *Humans* = the named role holders (S10 cards). *Agents* = automations and inference agents, with the explicit silence rule (spec §11.19-C fixes 27/28: mode-aware triggers; backfill-flagged events never trigger agent actions; `disaster`/`downtime` context pauses clinical drafters). *Paper* = the downtime kit form kinds and reserved serial ranges (Plan 11c/11d today: `registration|consultation|receipt`; the six kit kinds Plan 30 adds: `session_slip`, `diet_slip`, `transport_slip`, `gate_log`, `body_receipt`, `turnover_slip`, plus per-module pads — paper MAR, paper Form F, paper MLC serials, paper GRN, paper custody form). *Backfill* = entry with `occurred_at` ≠ `recorded_at`, `late_entry.flagged`, second-person close of the window. *Audit next day* = the query an assessor, a court or the owner runs.

## 2. Hospital-wide network outage, 3 hours mid-shift — then the reconciliation morning

**Setting.** Tuesday 14:00–17:10, core VM reachable from nowhere (stage 1 = one cloud VM; the building's WAN link is down, not the VM). 180 beds live, OPD afternoon session, pharmacy counter with 40 waiting, lab mid-run, a lap tubectomy open in the mini-OT, dialysis evening shift starting, a payment run approved but unsent, and a death on ward 3 at 15:40.

**14:00–14:05 — declaring.** Nobody can emit `downtime.declared`; the duty manager declares on paper with a second person (D-28; 16 6.1) and PBX-broadcasts. Heartbeats on every interface go stale; when the WAN returns the ops module will backfill the declaration with `occurred_at` 14:02. **Because Plan 30 (floor-scoped downtime) is not built, this is a hospital-wide declaration** — every floor is on paper even though the fault is one WAN link.

## 3. A VIP, an MLC, a fraud attempt and a DPDP data-principal request in one afternoon

**Audit next day.** Access-vs-care-relationship report for the VIP (§11.19-E-29); MLC register row with intimation proof; the suppressed-name attempt as an evented refusal; incident + insurer notification for the identity fraud; the bank-change request in cooling-off with no payment; the DSR row with export hash and the refusal reason; zero agent actions on sealed records outside the treating team.

## 5. Doctors' strike / mass leave — residents run everything

**Setting.** A state-wide resident doctors' association calls a strike from 08:00 Monday over a violence incident at another hospital; 60 % of the hospital's JR/SR pool joins; consultants (visiting panel) are present but thin; a "sympathy" half-day by nursing unions is rumoured (10 6.5). The hospital does not close.

**07:00 — the roster substrate is the whole game.** Plan 20's on-duty picture shows published slots with no `duty.checked_in` (10 §14 negative space); Coverage Resolver (T3) proposes: consultants take first-call on their own inpatients, RMOs cover ward escalation ladders, the ICU floor intensivist takes hall 2 and 3, locums from the S10 §12.4 pool on `temp_role` grants ≤ 90 d with privileges from the credential registry only (R-113). The duty manager approves the plan; the MS declares `surge_mode` (S10 §12.4). The escalation ladder (43) re-resolves every rung to the on-duty holder — never a named person (§11.12) — so the 03:00 potassium still reaches somebody.

**What changes by module.** *OPD:* doctor planned-leave cascade fires per absent doctor (L6): book blocks, auto-notify, one-tap rebooking, unresolved → call tasks; overbooking policy suspended (R-216). *IPD:* rounds worklists compress to consultant-only; `round.patient_seen` KPI denominators drop (load context, R-123); discharge summaries — the Discharge Drafter stays at T2 and the *consultant* signs (R-192 delegation is unavailable because the RMO is on strike, so the countersign window becomes the sign). *ED:* one ER physician per shift; triage nurse cannot downgrade Red (card 40 rule); Green-to-OPD conversion suspended outside OPD hours (R-149); boarding escalates faster — `bed_request` T1 escalation hits the MS at 60 min. *OT:* elective lists cancelled with reason `staff_unavailable` → hospital-attributable cost centre (R-165), no patient charge; emergency theatre only. *Verbal orders:* volume triples; countersign window (R-078) will be breached — the system records the breach and escalates to HoD/MS, it does not block care. *Narcotics:* the JR-blocked signing matrix (R-109) is unaffected — SR+ or consultant signs; if neither is reachable within the ladder, the nurse records under the emergency profile and the countersign is chased. *Pharmacy:* verification queue (16e) throttles to continuation-order auto-verification (R-180) and human review of new orders by the clinical pharmacist. *MCCD:* the certifier chain (R-111) collapses to consultant → MS; a night death waits for the consultant's telephone concurrence — the 4-h target may slip, and the chain SLA breach is recorded.

**Humans.** MS runs the day; matron manages the nursing rumour with the roster gates (a shift without a witness does not publish — S10 §11); HR SaaS records the strike leave; the owner speaks to the RDA representative; security prepares for a protest at the gate (Code Violet readiness, 07 O-11 policy). Nobody is punished by a number: KPIs for the window are excluded from composites (R-123) and the S10 non-punitive law applies.

**Agents.** Coverage Resolver is the hero — but it stays T3: every proposal is a human approval. Consult-Request Router (43) routes to consultants directly.
