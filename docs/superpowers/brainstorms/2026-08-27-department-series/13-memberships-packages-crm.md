# 13 — Memberships, Loyalty, Corporate & Insurance Tie-ups, Health Check-up Packages, Channel Partners & CRM — Brainstorm & Planning

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Status:** Brainstorm v1 — for owner review; nothing here is ruled unless marked RULED.
**Series note:** "13" is this brainstorm series' number; it is unrelated to roadmap **Plan 13 (resource registry)**. Proposed plan numbers for this content are 20–23 (§14).
**Context files referenced by path only (out of git):** `/opt/hmis-context/plan-09-channel-partners-2026-08-23.md` (synthetic partner book), `/opt/hmis-context/plan-09-brainstorm-2026-08-25.md`. No partner code, plan code, rate or sample person from either appears here or may appear in `apps/` (Plan 09 DD3).

**Executive summary.** This module family is the hospital's *commercial relationship layer*: every arrangement under which someone other than the walk-in self-payer influences what is charged, who pays, or why the patient comes back — memberships and programme cards, prepaid bundles, health check-up packages, corporate and society tie-ups, corporate credit accounts, TPA/insurer and government-scheme panels (PMJAY/CGHS/ECHS/ESIC), channel partners and referrers, camps, and the patient CRM (segments, recalls, feedback, grievance, consent). What is BUILT (Plan 09 + 09a, live and inert in production): tiered membership plans as config, membership instances with covered members, entitlement counters with an append-only movement log, coupon definitions and redemptions, the holder-book import lane with quarantine and reconcile queue, the counterparty/agreement model with `external_rmp` un-payable by composite FK, the accrual ledger (payable + receivable) as an event consumer with replay, statement reconciliation and aging, and five structural-OFF flags. It is NOT: a wallet or prepaid-payment instrument (RBI PPI exposure is designed out), a clinical module (a health check-up *order* is a Plan 17/18 order bundle; this module owns the package, the schedule and the collation), a claims engine (adjudication is the insurer's), or a marketing SaaS (WhatsApp/SMS transport is Plan 10's; templates and DLT registration are bought). **Three hardest problems:** (1) *the money-law boundary* — trust-hospital §11(4A) income, GST on vouchers/memberships/packages, TDS 194H on partner commissions, RBI PPI on anything that looks like stored value, and cut-practice under IMC cl. 6.4 — every one gates a lane and none is a code fix; (2) *payer multiplicity on one bill* — corporate credit, TPA sanction, scheme package rate, membership benefit and a coupon can all be true for one patient at once, and the adjustment contest, the payer-share decomposition and the credit ledger must agree to the paisa without stacking; (3) *consent-bound engagement at 2,000 OPD/day* — recalls, renewals, birthdays and NPS are cheap to send and expensive to send wrongly (DPDP withdrawal, TRAI DLT, quiet hours, sealed patients, deceased members), so the CRM must be a consent-checked read model over the spine, never a contact list.

## 3. Core flows as workflow definitions

All are workflow definitions (versioned data, §10.2); owner activates. Pattern mapping shown; SLAs are corporate-standard defaults, configurable.

**3.1 Programme membership instance (extends Plan 09; P6 + P7).**
```
[quoted] --sell/import--> [active] --expiry-30d--> [renewal_due] --renewed--> [active]
   |                         |  \--suspended(fraud/dispute)--> [suspended] --cleared--> [active]
   |                         \--expired--> [lapsed] --winback(90d)--> [closed]
   \--cooling_off_refund(≤7d, no benefit consumed)--> [refunded]
[active] --holder_deceased--> [frozen_estate] --heir_decision--> [transferred|refunded|closed]
```
Roles: sell/renew = Programme Desk (flag-gated); suspend = Billing Supervisor with reason; refund = approval `membership_refund` (approver billing_manager, ≠ seller). SLA: `renewal_due` 30 d → nudge ladder (T1, §9); `frozen_estate` 90 d → task to Programme Desk. Events: existing `membership.sold`, `membership.benefit_consumed`; NEW `membership.renewed`, `membership.lapsed`, `membership.suspended`, `membership.refunded`, `membership.upgraded`, `membership.member_added`, `membership.member_removed`, `membership.frozen`.

**3.2 Group membership (family / society / school / corporate) — a `membership_group` above instances.** Group = sponsor (patient, society, employer) × plan × roster × billing mode (`sponsor_pays | member_pays | split`). Lifecycle `draft → active → renewal_due → active | lapsed`; roster changes are movements (`group.member_joined/.left` NEW) never edits; corporate roster arrives as a bulk feed (Plan 09 import lane reused, column-map versioned). Employee exit = `member_left` with `effective_on`; benefit continues to `grace_days` (default 0 for corporate-paid, plan expiry for employee-paid — **O-6**).

**3.3 Chronic-care club (diabetes / dialysis / cardiac / renal / ANC).** A programme = plan + *care calendar template* + counters. Enrol (doctor recommends, patient consents, pays or sponsor pays) → `active` → sessions consumed against counters via §11.4 map 11's day-care flow → `missed_session` is a *clinical recall* not a no-show (locked) → `graduated | lapsed | transferred`. Clinical content (protocols, targets) belongs to the clinical module; this module owns enrolment, entitlement, adherence read model and renewals. Events NEW: `programme.enrolled`, `programme.session_missed` (consumes `task.escalated`), `programme.graduated`.

**3.4 Health check-up package day (P2 bundle + P5 + P6).**
```
[booked] --T-1 fasting call/WA--> [instructed] --arrival scan--> [checked_in]
 --stations routed (lab draw → ECG → X-ray → USG → consults)--> [in_progress]
 --all results published--> [collated] --doctor review slot--> [reviewed]
 --report signed + QR + summary letter--> [delivered] --abnormal? recall--> [followed_up]
[booked] --no_show--> [rescheduled|cancelled(refund rule)]
[in_progress] --component undeliverable (machine down)--> [partial] --resume/credit note--> [collated]
```
SLA: `checked_in→in_progress` 15 min; `in_progress` fasting samples drawn ≤ 30 min of arrival; `collated` by 17:00 same day for basic tiers, T+1 for imaging-heavy; `reviewed` ≤ 24 h; `delivered` ≤ 48 h (corporate SLA often 72 h). Escalation: coordinator → lab manager → billing supervisor (for partial credits) → MS (for review backlog). Package is priced as ONE invoice line with `package_id`; component orders carry `package_component_of` so the orphan report (§11.11) treats them as covered, not missed revenue. Events: existing `package.applied`, `package.allowance_consumed`, `package.overrun_projected`, `order.placed`, `result.published`, `report.signed`; NEW `package.day_scheduled`, `package.instruction_sent`, `package.collated`, `package.partially_delivered`, `package.report_delivered`.

**3.5 Corporate bulk check-up (P4-shaped for the employer side + 3.4 per employee).** Employer request → quote (tier × headcount × on-site vs in-hospital) → `approved` → slot block (registry rooms/benches, Plan 13) → roster import → per-employee 3.4 instances → employer consolidated invoice (credit account, 3.6) → **employer report** (aggregate only; individual reports go to the employee; 17(2) perquisite statement on request) → `closed`. Events NEW: `corporate_booking.requested/.approved/.roster_imported/.report_issued`.

**3.6 Corporate / payer credit account (P6 credit control, §11.11 verbatim).**
```
[proposed] --owner approves limit--> [active] --utilisation ≥80%--> [warning] --≥100%--> [over_limit]
[active] --invoice unpaid 15d--> reminder --30d--> reminder --45d--> call task --60d--> [credit_stopped]
[credit_stopped] --payment or management override (evented, owner digest)--> [active]
[any] --terminated--> [closing] --zero balance--> [closed]
```
Roles: limit = owner approval (`credit_limit_grant` NEW approval type); override = management, evented `credit.stop_overridden` (NEW); write-off = approvals ladder, `writeoff.recorded`. Aging computes on *net-of-TDS expectation* (D-A 2). Monthly consolidated invoice = `statement.issued`.

**3.7 TPA / insurer cashless episode (commercial lens; clinical docs from IPD).**
```
[intimated] --docs complete--> [preauth_sent] --insurer query--> [query_open] --reply--> [preauth_sent]
 --approved--> [sanctioned(amount, class, LOS, scope)] --clinical deviation/limit 80%--> [enhancement_sent]
 --approved--> [sanctioned] ; --denied--> [payer_switch_counselling] (map 3) --> self-pay path
[sanctioned] --discharge intimation + final bill + docs--> [discharge_clearance_sent] --approved--> [cleared]
 --> [claim_submitted] --settlement--> [settled(gross/disallowed/TDS/net)] | [short_paid] --reconcile/appeal--> [settled|written_off]
[claim_submitted] --denied--> [denied] --appeal(≤ payer window)--> [appealed] --> [settled|written_off]
```
SLA (corporate-standard): intimation → pre-auth sent ≤ 2 h (planned) / ≤ 6 h (emergency, insurer norm 24 h); query reply ≤ 4 h working; enhancement requested before 80% of sanction consumed (S10 #5 KPI); discharge clearance sent within 1 h of final bill; claim submitted ≤ 7 d post-discharge; aging buckets 0–30/31–60/61–90/>90 with insurer-level escalation at 45 d. Ladder: TPA exec → billing supervisor → MS (clinical query) → owner (write-off). Events: existing `preauth.denied`, `payer.switched`, `preauth.deviation_flagged`, `settlement.recorded`, `statement.disputed`; NEW `preauth.requested`, `preauth.queried`, `preauth.sanctioned`, `enhancement.requested`, `discharge_clearance.requested/.granted`, `claim.submitted`, `claim.denied`, `claim.appealed`, `claim.short_paid`. NHCX later: same states, transport changes (FHIR claim bundle) — do not fork the definition.

**3.8 Government scheme episode (PMJAY / CGHS / ECHS / ESIC).** `beneficiary_verified (ABHA/BIS e-KYC + photo) → package_blocked (HBP code, rate snapshot) → preauth (mandatory for reserved packages) → treated → discharge docs (photo/discharge summary uploaded on TMS) → claim → settled | rejected | audit_query → responded`. Scheme package rate is an *adjustment source* with `payer_scope = scheme` and a **hard cap**: no self-pay top-up beyond NHA rules except explicitly allowed items; PMJAY forbids charging the beneficiary anything — enforced as a billing refusal, not a warning. ESIC: referral letter number mandatory; CGHS/ECHS: card + referral validity + rate list version. Audit register first-class (§7).

**3.9 Channel partner & referrer attribution (built; extended).** Add `referrer_kind ∈ channel_partner | staff_internal | external_rmp | agent_promoter | ambulance_operator | chemist` — the last three are payee class (b) "lawful marketing spend" only if counsel says so (**O-2**); external RMP stays composite-FK un-payable. Camp partners ride `counterparties`.

**3.10 Camp → lead → conversion (P7 over P1).** `camp.planned → kit_issued → conducted (screenings recorded offline, batch-synced) → leads_captured → coupons_issued (flag) → converted (first hospital visit linked by lead_id) → closed`. Lead lifecycle `new → contacted → booked → visited → converted | dropped(reason) | do_not_contact`. Events NEW: `camp.planned`, `camp.conducted`, `lead.captured`, `lead.contacted`, `lead.converted`, `lead.dropped`.

**3.11 Feedback / NPS and grievance ladder.** Feedback: `requested (post-visit, quiet-hours aware) → received(score, text) → triaged (T2 draft) → closed | escalated_to_grievance`. Grievance (§11.14 locked): `raised → acknowledged (≤ 24 h) → investigating (owner: rung 1 CRM exec, rung 2 dept head, rung 3 QM, rung 4 MS/owner) → resolved → acknowledged_by_patient → closed`; statutory: Clinical Establishments (state rules) grievance display, NABH PRE chapter register, DPDP grievance officer TAT (DPDP Rules: response within prescribed period; default 7 d internal, escalate at 3 d). Events existing `grievance.raised/.resolved`; NEW `feedback.requested/.received/.triaged`, `grievance.acknowledged`, `grievance.escalated`, `review.responded`.

**3.12 Consent & contactability (the gate every flow above passes).** Not a lifecycle of its own — a *predicate*: `contactable(patient, purpose, channel, now)` = alive ∧ not sealed-for-purpose ∧ consent(purpose) ∧ channel opt-in ∧ not do-not-disturb ∧ (urgent ∨ outside quiet hours) ∧ DLT template registered. Withdrawal → `consent.withdrawn` (NEW) → Consent Propagator automation cancels queued promotional messages within one sweep and tombstones segments.

ED treats unconditionally; admission request under the corporate is refused by the ledger; duty manager overrides (evented, owner real-time alert), patient admitted; morning: Corporate Relations calls, payment plan; override appears in the weekly digest with the authoriser's name. No clinical delay — the override is a one-tap approval on the admission clerk's screen.

## 10. Speed, accuracy, efficiency, auditability — the levers

- **One beep:** card/QR on the membership slip, employee QR on the corporate day sheet, scheme card scan → full context; no typing of card numbers (alphabet without 0/O/1/I/L is already the coupon rule).
- **Keyboard-first counter:** recognition pops as a non-blocking banner with the contest result visible ("Gold 15% won over coupon ₹200 — both recorded"); F-keys for grace-honor request.
- **Check-up day board:** wall screen per station (token only), colour by fasting/next station; phlebotomist tablet marks draws; collation auto-flips when the last `result.published` lands; doctor-review slots pre-booked at scheduling. Targets: arrival→first draw < 15 min; basic tier delivered same day ≥ 90%.
- **Pre-filled pre-auth:** Claims Drafter fills from encounter; the desk edits, never types; document checklist per payer with red/green; TAT clocks on the worklist. Targets: planned pre-auth sent < 2 h p90; claim submitted ≤ 7 d 95%.
- **Payer share on every bill preview:** patient sees insurer share / self share / non-payables before signing; upgrade shows the cap deduction.
- **Renewal in two taps:** signed short-lived link (public read surface, E-1) → UPI → instance extends; desk sees it live.
- **Print as design surface:** package report with QR verification, summary letter in patient language, employer report aggregate with n-suppression note, membership terms in Hindi/English.
- **Voice where lawful:** IVR fasting instructions and renewal reminders for feature-phone patients; recorded consent playback logged; no voice capture of PHI into agents.
- **Auditability:** every benefit, rate substitution, payer decomposition and consent check is a row or event with actor; contest candidates recorded including losers (D-8); statement/settlement rows carry UTR and TDS; every agent draft stamped with provenance.
