## 1. North-star parameters

### G1 — the measured wait · **amend**
1. **`called_at − eligible_at` is not the hall wait.** `eligibleAt` is RESET to `now` on every non-leaving skip (`modules/opd/queue.ts:159`: `eligibleAt: left ? current.eligibleAt : now`) and `calledAt` is overwritten on every call (`:131`, `callCount + 1`). A patient who stepped out once and was re-called measures "since the last skip", not "since arrival"; p90 is biased low exactly for the long waits. Nearest real: events — `patient.checked_in` (`opd/events.ts:132`) or the first `waiting` transition → the FIRST `queue.called` (`:304`, `callCount = 1`). Define it there, or say the column metric is "wait since last eligibility".
2. **"MAE of quoted vs realised" has no record of the quote.** Nothing persists what was quoted: the number is computed in the browser (`model.ts:238-240`, `walk-in-routing.ts:56`) from a wire that carries only `waitingCount` and `avgConsultMinutes` (`queue.ts:340-346`); `updateDepartment` writes no event (`modules/opd/masters.ts:110-118`, no `appendEvent` in the file), so even the constant's history is unrecoverable. A backtest can replay a FORMULA against the event log; it cannot recover the quote. Either persist it (`wait.quoted {entryId, minutes, paceSource, paceN}` at check-in, or a `quoted_wait_minutes` column on `opd_queue_entries`) or rename the parameter "formula replay vs realised".
3. **"the owner's digest line" is a hypothetical reader**: no digest exists — "digests are 12a producers, not this plan" (`kernel/notify/pump.ts:109`). Nearest real readers: `owner_escalation_sms` (`kernel/notify/templates.ts:126`) or the `desk.opd.*` tiles (`modules/opd/desk-provider.ts:35-47`).
4. **Two thresholds for one goal, unreconciled**: the KPI spec's OKR is *wait-SLA > 92 %* on `sla.breached` (spec `:40-42`), which fires at **45 min** (`workflow-def.ts:21`); G1's target is *p90 ≤ 30 min*. A session can be 100 % SLA-compliant and fail G1. Say which is the goal and which the guard.

### G3 — the card and the counted drafts · **amend**
1. **"0 sections `UNAVAILABLE` per consult" / "≤ 1 % `UNAVAILABLE`" is recorded nowhere.** The four states are computed on read and returned (16b DD3 `:64`), refetched every 15 s (DD5 `:66`), never stored; `phi_access_log` holds `patient_id, surface, encounter_id, context, reason, at` (`schema/phi-access.ts:50-80`) — no section states. A `phi_access_log` row with surface `briefing` proves the READ happened, not what state the sections rendered in. Minimal addition (16b T2): a `briefing.assembled {encounterId, packVersion, states: Record<section, state>}` event, or `unavailable_sections` on the briefing audit row.
2. **"the DTC" and "the steward" are not roles anywhere** (`scripts/seed-roles.ts`: no such keys); the "who reads it" column names people who do not exist in the system. Name a role that does (`department_head` for templates; the owner for drafts) or mark as "to be created in 16b-ii".
3. Rest is honestly labelled: F22's denominator (`16a:378-381` confirmed), the three override counts (`opd/events.ts:333-342` real numerators), the scribe events new.
