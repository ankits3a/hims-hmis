## C. Agent capability, inference-before-DPIA, fail-open, API-only / provenance / kill switch (lens 3)

### C1 · E4 walks past the DPIA's Class-2 design law and R-254/R-126's on-prem default — **amend**

### C4 · `npo.extended` fires on the *proposal*, before any human accepts — **amend (safety)**
