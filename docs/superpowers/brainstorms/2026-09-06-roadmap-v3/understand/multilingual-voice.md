# multilingual-voice — languages and speech: what exists, what binds, what a ladder looks like

Read-only survey of `origin/main` @ `b04cbd9` (paths relative to the main-ro checkout). Mission pillar 1b ("understand and process speech across 15 Indian languages natively") and 2b (a listening ambient copilot) are measured against the repo, the department series, the rulings register, the DPIA and ROADMAP v2. Status vocabulary: **deployed** = in `c11833d` (the production base) · **merged** = on `main` only · **authored** = in a plan/brainstorm · **absent**.

## 0. The one-paragraph answer

Two languages exist end to end (Hindi, English) and they are type- and test-enforced at every surface the OS owns: UI strings, outbound patient messages, the server-rendered OPD papers and the lab report. No third language can even be *recorded*: `patients.language` is `'hi' | 'en'` (`apps/core/src/kernel/db/schema/patients.ts:95`), the registration picker offers exactly those two (`apps/web/src/screens/desk-one/stages.tsx:787-792`), and the speech hint is `"hi" | "en"` (`apps/core/src/kernel/inference/types.ts:17`). Speech exists as one inert route (`POST /speech/transcribe`, deployed, answers 503) plus two inert front ends (palette microphone, consult scribe). Everything past push-to-talk transcription is blocked by a standing design law ("voice audio is Class 2 until the DPIA rules otherwise") that only the owner (accept the DPIA §3-A carve-out) and counsel (DPIA v0.2) can lift. No document names fifteen languages; the series names Bhojpuri 36 times, Maithili 3, Bengali 2, Punjabi 1.

## 1. ui-i18n — DEPLOYED (hi/en), parity-tested; no server-side locale

**What exists.**
- `apps/web/src/locales/en.json` and `hi.json`: 3,667 lines, 3,065 leaves each; `hi.json` is in `c11833d`, so the Hindi UI is deployed. Of the 3,065 Hindi leaves, **67 contain no Devanagari and 66 are byte-identical to English** — all acronyms/units (`HMIS`, `UHID`, `ABHA`, `PMJAY`, `SBP (mmHg)`, `SpO₂`, `IV/IM/SC`). That 67 is a usable baseline for a "no untranslated string" parameter.
- Runtime: `apps/web/src/lib/i18n.ts:8-13` — i18next, language from `localStorage["hmis.lang"]`, default and fallback `en`; `:28-38` stamps `<html lang>` (a screen reader otherwise spoke Devanagari in an English voice). Toggles at `login.tsx:303` and `router.tsx:357`; `switchLanguage(lng: "en" | "hi")` — the type is the language list.
- Parity tests: `apps/web/src/lib/i18n.test.ts:11-13` (hi ≡ en key-for-key); `i18n-keys.test.ts` (8 tests: every literal `t("ns.key")` resolves in `en.json`, plurals, the "`1 patient(s)`" class — `:7-39`, `:143-150`). `kernel/notify/templates.ts:20` makes a template missing a language **fail to compile**.
- Design law: spec §15 "Hindi/English switchable UI day one" (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-10-hmis-architecture-design.md:771`); the 16a plan's F18 — an English-only string leaves a Hindi user with a raw key on a safety warning (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-08-25-phase1-16a-formulary-prescribing-safety.md:571-574`).
- Commissioning: 11i §2b row 21 "Staff who read Hindi better than English → T6 runs one seat in Hindi and records any untranslated string as a defect" (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-06-phase1-11i-the-stand-up-path.md:111`, `:296-300`). That is the first *measured* Hindi artefact the project will have; nobody has run a seat in Hindi on UAT yet (T6 is open, #117-#122 per CONTEXT).

**Gaps.**
- Server refusals are English prose: `apps/core/src/modules/lab/lab-desk.controller.ts:81,84` (zod messages), `apps/core/src/modules/lab/lab-http.ts:74` (raw zod issues thrown to the client). The good pattern exists elsewhere — `materials.rejectReason` carries a `RuleCode` and "the screen renders its locale string" (`apps/core/src/kernel/db/schema/materials.ts:739`; `apps/web/src/lib/error-strings.test.ts`). The lane memory "refusals are prose carrying data" records why a naive fix regresses the lab's messages (digits read the same in both scripts).
- No per-user (staff) language column exists in any schema (grep `preferredLanguage|ui_language|locale` over `kernel/db/schema` → only the materials comment). UI language is a browser-local choice, not an identity attribute — fine for a shared desk, wrong for a personal mobile worklist (10 §9 Lane 1).
- A third language is a 3,065-key translation job per language on a file every lane edits (CLAUDE.md), not a config flag.

## 8. What "natively" can honestly mean under buy-not-build (spec §9) and the DPIA

- Spec §9 buys commodity infrastructure and licenses clinical knowledge "never authored" (`spec:158-166`); every model sits behind `InferenceClient`/`SpeechClient`, swappable by config (`types.ts`, `11h:356-359`); 12 §9.11 pins models per playbook, cloud today, on-prem when residency or evals demand (`12:538-542`). So **"natively" cannot mean a model the hospital trained, and must not mean "the cloud does it" for clinical audio** (`12:514`).
- The defensible reading: *native = the OS is language-neutral at every surface it owns, and the language is an attribute of the patient/staff/document, not of the deployment.* Concretely: a BCP-47 tag (with a spoken-vs-read split — a Bhojpuri speaker reads Hindi, `20:427`) on patient, user, document and display; a template registry keyed by language with a logged fallback (`20:431`); per-language eval fixtures (`12:193`); and ASR/TTS/MT bought behind the choke module with a measured WER per language on the hospital's own recordings before any language is switched on (`voice-flag.ts:12-18`, `11h:688`). Candidates at procurement time (model knowledge, not in the repo — verify): the pinned Whisper large-v3 (covers hi/bn/ta/te/mr/gu/kn/ml/pa/ur/ne/as/sd/sa, not Bhojpuri/Maithili), Bhashini / AI4Bharat IndicConformer (22 scheduled languages, on-prem-able), Sarvam's Indic ASR — the owner's procurement ruling, one line.
- The DPIA fixes the shape regardless of vendor: audio is the only PHI that cannot be tokenised before it leaves (`11h:301-304`), so every speech epic is a carve-out — named processor, stated location, nothing persisted, per-use audit — that "closes when in-region or on-prem ASR arrives" (`dpia:33`). Ambient adds a second data principal (the patient), so a consent artefact in *their* language per consultation.

## 9. A realistic epic ladder (letters inside existing blocks; numbering frozen)

Each rung: goal → parameter that proves it (measurable from a row/event/runbook) → gate → the ruling it needs. Edge rows follow 11i §2b's shape.

**L1 — UI i18n hardening (11i T6 + a small front-desk/kernel slice; this quarter).** Goal: a seat run in Hindi has zero raw keys and zero English refusals. Gate: UAT. Ruling: none (DECIDED).
- Edge: pathologist verifies in the Hindi UI, report prints English (NABL) → `lang` param unchanged → artefact: both copies from one screen.

Parameters: migration widening `patients.language` to a tag + `spoken_language` (additive; `patients` is a shared module — coordinate); `notification.sent` rows by language vs `patients.language` distribution (one SQL); `language_fallback` event count; IVR rung exists in `adapters.ts` (today 2 channels → 3). ## Surprises

1. **The scribe drops the language hint.** `consult-scribe.tsx:85` posts `{ audio }` only; the route defaults `language` to `"en"` (`speech.controller.ts:33`). The palette path was specified to pass the i18n language (`11h:525-526`). Invisible while the route answers 503; a real defect on the day it does not.
2. **The amendment the flag waits for was never written.** Every artefact says "until the amendment is recorded in spec §19" — the spec's §19 has no such text; the DPIA §3-A row is the only place it exists, marked pending. The gate is a paragraph the owner has not accepted, not a build.
3. **The schema cannot record the hospital's own second language.** Bhojpuri is the most-named language in the department series and `patients.language` is `'hi' | 'en'`. "15 languages" starts with a migration on a module every lane imports.
4. **Speech *output* is already live and nobody lists it as voice.** The OPD display has spoken every called token in Hindi and English since Plan 07 — the mission's "speech" today is a browser TTS call, not a model.
5. **The designed alternative to dictation-to-draft is a person** — the `SCRIBE` post (card 42, `10:65`; `10:508`). Under R-059 the cheapest ambient copilot is a human with a tablet.
6. **Print's Hindi depends on a font on the relay.** `render.ts:89-91` — without `Noto Sans Devanagari` a slip prints boxes; no test can see it.
7. **One counsel session is the critical path for the whole speech pillar.** The DPIA draft has not moved since 2026-08-25 and voice, 12a, 43c and 44's Drafter all wait on it (ROADMAP v2 `:405`).
