# The owner's mission brief (verbatim, 2026-09-06) — the north star this brainstorm serves

What we are building here is an Agentic-AI-run hospital operating system that has AI agents as assistant/co-pilot to human users. Our OS manages outpatient consultations, diagnostics, surgeries, inpatient care, inventory, and discharge processes through AI-driven workflows. It integrates clinical data, hospital infrastructure, and staff coordination into a unified system that assigns tasks to both AI agents and human teams in real time. In outpatient departments, the system adjusts appointment durations based on visit type and functions as an ambient AI during consultations. It surfaces prior patient records, suggests clinical considerations, and drafts prescriptions for physician review and approval. It also coordinates medicines and lab sample collection directly within consultation rooms. Our OS should be integrated into radiology and pathology workflows through a cloud-based imaging infrastructure. We have to eliminate traditional PACS servers at the facility, enabling instant image transfer and AI-supported scan analysis. In its pivotal diagnostic workflows, the OS should perform 3D volumetric analysis across imaging modalities, including intracranial hemorrhage mapping, fracture detection, implant positioning, and pneumothorax identification. For inpatient and surgical workflows, the OS should be able to coordinate operating room schedules, surgeon availability, anesthesia planning, equipment allocation, and room assignments. During procedures, it should monitor timelines and adjust schedules dynamically if delays occur. The system should also generate discharge summaries instantly for physician approval. The OS should include patient monitoring capabilities that adjust alerts based on individual clinical histories rather than fixed thresholds. Additional beta features should include automated drug–drug interaction checks and AI-based post-surgical wound assessment through remote image scans.

## 1. Unified Intelligence Fabric & Dual Workflows
- **Agentic Task Orchestration:** connect every hospital department into a single intelligence layer. Assign real-time clinical and operational tasks to both AI agents and human staff (nurses, phlebotomists, radiology technicians, care coordinators).
- **Multilingual Capability:** understand and process speech across 15 Indian languages natively.

## 2. Intelligent Outpatient Department (OPD)
- **Zero-Wait Dynamic Scheduling:** adjust appointment lengths dynamically by visit type (initial consultation, follow-up, post-surgical review) and predict potential delays in advance.
- **Ambient AI Co-Pilot:** an ambient assistant during consultations — listens in real time, surfaces relevant medical history, suggests clinical considerations, auto-drafts prescriptions for doctor approval.
- **In-Consultation Service Delivery:** coordinate blood sample collection and medicine delivery directly inside the consultation room, eliminating pharmacy and lab queues.

## 3. Diagnostic & Surgical Orchestration
- **Fibre-to-Cloud Radiology:** replace traditional in-house PACS servers with high-speed fibre infrastructure, transmitting imaging directly to our cloud for instant AI annotation and 3D volumetric analysis across neurology, orthopedics, oncology, and chest trauma.
- **Dynamic Operating Theatre (OT) Management:** analyse surgeon availability, OT capacity, procedure type, anaesthesia and equipment needs to auto-schedule and continuously rebalance surgical timelines in real time if delays occur.

## 4. Patient Care & Monitoring
- **Personalised Patient Monitoring:** replace generic alert thresholds with customised baselines (e.g. a long-distance runner's low resting heart rate is not an alarm) while still detecting true deviations.
- **Medication Safety Checks (Beta):** screen digital prescriptions for drug–drug interactions in real time before adverse events.
- **Vision AI Post-Surgical Healing (Beta):** discharged patients upload wound images via the web app; Vision AI evaluates stitch integrity, scab formation, signs of infection.
- **Physiotherapy Trajectory Tracking (Beta):** track pre-/post-surgery range of motion for joint replacements; alert clinical teams if recovery strays from the expected trajectory.

## 5. Operational & Quality Assurance
- **"Magic Discharge":** instantly generate the summary on doctor confirmation and process departmental clearances automatically in the background.
- **100% Case Real-Time Auditing:** monitor and audit 100% of patient interactions against clinical protocols in real time.
