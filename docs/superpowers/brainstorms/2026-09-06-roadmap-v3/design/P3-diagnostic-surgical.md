## 0. The mission sentence(s) this pillar serves, and the honest reconciliation with the spec

**3a:** *"replace traditional in-house PACS servers with high-speed fibre … transmitting imaging directly to our cloud for instant AI annotation and 3D volumetric analysis across neurology, orthopedics, oncology, and chest trauma"* — *"intracranial hemorrhage mapping, fracture detection, implant positioning, pneumothorax identification"*. **3b:** *"analyse surgeon availability, OT capacity, procedure type, anaesthesia and equipment needs to auto-schedule and continuously rebalance surgical timelines in real time if delays occur"*.

1. **"Eliminate in-house PACS servers" inverts the owner's own ruling.** spec:24-33 (v4.7, 2026-08-22): *"Hosted on local (on-premises) servers. Patient care must never depend on internet connectivity. THIS IS THE END STATE … No managed cloud service becomes load-bearing."* The WAN-down day relies on *"modalities keep acquiring (edge-local DICOM to Orthanc)"* (`00-CROSS-MODULE-CHAOS.md:42`); 18b §7 R1 recommends an on-prem Orthanc under 11b (18b:88). Nobody has costed a cloud-primary PACS. **Reconciled goal:** *"cloud" is the offsite tier and the AI tier, never the acquisition edge* — images land on-prem, replicate to an India-region object store from day one (doc01 O-9 `:578`; R-254 REG:266), and leave only through a governed outbound register. Only the owner can reverse v4.7; §5 puts three priced options to him (NEW-P3-1).
2. **"Instant transfer" is procurement plus one measured parameter.** `fibre|bandwidth|Mbps` occur nowhere in the repo; the only number is storage growth (doc01:564: ~150 MB/study, 100/day ≈ 15 GB/day). HMIS measures acquisition→viewable (`imaging.study_acquired` → first `imaging.image_viewed`, `modules/radiology/events.ts:66,105`) once images flow; the link, its redundancy and the MWL licences are purchases (18b R2 `:89`).
3. **"AI annotation" is bought, T1 first, T2 forever.** R-008 (REG:20): *"evaluate CXR-triage/stroke tools only as T1 nudges after PACS is stable; bought never built."* spec:778 caps clinical actions at T2–T3 permanently. The only inference seam is `SpeechClient` (`kernel/inference/types.ts:32`); `InferenceClient` is three comments; `kernel/copilot` does not exist; RM §5:428 excludes 12a. A machine finding is a worklist hint + a row, never a report sentence (18b D7; `docs/runbooks/radiology-pacs-go-live.md:134-135`).
4. **"3D volumetric analysis" has no consumer.** MPR is a viewer capability (OHIF free, R3 18b:90); volumetric *measurement* is a vendor product's output. ICH and tension pneumothorax are **Red**, unexpected fracture **Orange** in the governed book (doc01:127); the consuming departments — ED (40), orthopaedics/neurology (no plan), chest trauma (40/67), major OT (48) — sit behind the IPD gate (RM §4:391-410; index §3:61). HMIS's part in every row: a structured field with `provenance` (`kernel/db/schema/radiology.ts:434`) in a T2 draft a radiologist signs — *machine-proposed, human-signed, edit-distance counted*.
5. **The DPIA has never heard of an image** (0 occurrences of image/DICOM/pixel/radiology). Its Class 1 (`DPIA:19`) is token-referenced text; L1 (`:28`) demands re-identification and DPDP §16 analysis before any Class-1 request. A head CT reconstructs a face; header de-id (doc01 J5 `:330`) is not pixel de-id. **Every byte that leaves — replica, teleradiology, AI processor — needs an imaging addendum that does not exist** (R-262 REG:274). Counsel.
6. **"Auto-schedule and rebalance in real time" splits into a rule and an agent.** doc15 §9 `:466-468` designed the **Overrun Cascade** (automation T1→T3, *"re-sequence proposal applied on in-charge accept"*, "ships with Plan 15" — it did not; routed to 15d by `…2026-08-28-plan-15-mini-ot/00-RECORD-AND-PLAN.md:214`) and the **OT List Optimiser** (agent T2, *"never publishes"*, Plan 48, *"mini-OT: simple heuristic suffices"*). spec:780: the rule wins where it is safer. **Reconciled goal:** a deterministic cascade that proposes, an in-charge who accepts; the inference optimiser waits on DPIA v0.2 and on median durations a unit with 498 production events cannot have.
7. **Availability and equipment are substrates.** Surgeon/anaesthetist availability is Plan 20 (DECIDED first Track C, RM:276-302); `modules/ot/lists.ts` is one of its five consumers (measured: `grep -rln usersHoldingRole apps/core/src` = `ot/lists.ts`, `workflow/timers.ts`, `notify/consumer.ts`, `workflow/roles.ts`, `alerts/consumer.ts`). Equipment is registry `device`, unclaimed by `ot` (`modules/ot/kinds.ts:47-51`), governed by Plan 29, deferred "until 15 opens for real" (RM §5:414-418).
8. **Absorption binds harder than building.** The mini-OT is deployed with no opening week on RM §2 (`:326-347`); radiology opens weeks 7–9 on certificates only the owner holds (`:338`); `standup:check` has no `ot` section (`apps/core/scripts/standup-check.ts:128,210,322,361`); no OT runbook exists (`ls docs/runbooks` = lab, pharmacy, radiation-safety, radiology-pacs). The pillar's first two epics are openings; every build epic names the human act it waits on.
