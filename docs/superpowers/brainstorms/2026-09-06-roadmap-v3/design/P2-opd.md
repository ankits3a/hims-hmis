## 0. The mission sentence(s) this pillar serves, and the honest reconciliation

**2a:** "adjust appointment lengths dynamically by visit type (initial consultation, follow-up, post-surgical review) and predict potential delays in advance." **2b:** "an ambient assistant during consultations — listens in real time, surfaces relevant medical history, suggests clinical considerations, auto-drafts prescriptions for doctor approval." **2c:** "coordinate blood sample collection and medicine delivery directly inside the consultation room, eliminating pharmacy and lab queues."

- **2a is designable now with zero inference and no ruling.** Slot length is one number (`opd_config.slot_minutes` default 10, "owner decision", `apps/core/src/kernel/db/schema/opd.ts:23`; per-template `:103`; a fixed `step`, `modules/opd/slots.ts:22-25`). The visit type is a pure function computed at visit OPEN (`modules/opd/visit-type.ts:9-13`, called at `encounters.ts:110`), never at booking (`schema/opd.ts:140-152` carries no type). No post-surgical type exists (`events.ts:321`). The only pace term is a hand-typed department constant (`avg_consult_minutes` default 6, `schema/opd.ts:48-50`, edited at `masters.ts:113-114`): the server ships it (`queue.ts:346`) and two web files multiply it by `waitingCount` (`apps/web/src/screens/desk-one/model.ts:239`; `apps/web/src/lib/walk-in-routing.ts:56`). Every clock a predictor needs is already evented in production — `visit.opened` (`opd/events.ts:77`), `patient.checked_in` (`:132`), `queue_session.opened{scheduledStart}` (`:294-296`), `queue.called` (`:304`), `consultation.started/.completed{visitType}` (`:315-321`), `sla.breached` from the 45-min active `waiting` state (`modules/opd/workflow-def.ts:21`; `kernel/workflow/events.ts:21`) — and **nothing reads them** (Plan 21 absent; `sla.breached` write-only). **Reconciled goal:** measure the pace per doctor from the events; quote the wait to staff as minutes with an age and to patients as **position, never a single figure** (22c-D DD4, `plans/2026-08-28-phase1-22cD-appointment-journey.md:84`, which "predict delays" must obey); let the doctor declare a delay; size slots per visit type from measured medians, the 10-minute ruling the default until medians exist. "Dynamic" means *set from the last 30 days by config*, never re-ordered by a model: L1 queue discipline is locked (`20-front-office…md:26`) and the Appointment Optimiser is T2 after 90 days of baselines (22b, `00-INDEX-AND-SYNTHESIS.md:68`) — outside this quarter and the next.

- **2b splits into four rungs; only the first two are lawful now.** (i) *Surface history* is Plan 16b, the Context Assembler & Snapshot Card — "zero inference, zero DPIA gate" (`specs/2026-08-25-clinical-copilot-design.md:172`), still **PROVISIONAL, not approved** (`plans/2026-08-25-phase1-16b-context-assembler-snapshot-card.md:3`; §6 CLOSE empty `:166`); today the doctor has three history tabs (`apps/web/src/screens/opd-consult.tsx:170`) over 07d T1's readers (`modules/opd/history.ts:9-12`). (ii) *Suggest considerations* is, by design law 4, never origination — "the LLM narrates, never originates; hard safety alerts come only from deterministic checks" (`copilot-design.md:29`): a consideration is a deterministic check hit (allergy/DDI/duplicate — deployed, `modules/opd/prescriptions.ts:257-260`; second call site `pharmacy/verify.ts:185-205`), a **cited** restatement across fact-sheet lines (Lens Phase B; uncited claims dropped `:114`; five ordered gates `:125-129`), or a protocol prompt once machine-readable protocols exist (deferred note 12, `plans/2026-08-11-phase1-plan-series.md:463`). Free-form differential diagnosis is outside the T2 cap (spec §16 `:778`) and a SaMD question for counsel (`copilot-design.md:192`); **not built**. (iii) *Auto-draft prescriptions* has no roster row anywhere (spec `:785`; `00-INDEX:144`). Reconciled: a **deterministic pre-fill** — department templates keyed by diagnosis, applied as editable lines, checked by the same engine at issue — is buildable on 16a+16b with no DPIA gate; a *model* Rx drafter is parked behind the Lens gates plus a counsel opinion, and its acceptance metric (F22's missing denominator, `plans/2026-08-25-phase1-16a-formulary-prescribing-safety.md:378-381`) must exist first. (iv) *Listens in real time* is refused by every artefact: "voice audio is Class 2 until the DPIA rules otherwise" (`kernel/inference/speech.controller.ts:40-44`); push-to-hold ≤ 15 s, audio discarded, transcript never chained to a model (`apps/web/src/components/consult-scribe.tsx:30-44`); R-059 blocks every transcript-to-note agent until the spec §19 amendment is ruled (`00-OWNER-RULINGS-REGISTER.md:71`); brainstorm 12 §9.6 forbids cloud ASR for clinical narratives. The route answers 503; `VOICE_SEARCH_ENABLED=false` carries three written preconditions (`apps/web/src/lib/voice-flag.ts:6-27`). **Reconciled:** the co-pilot ladder is card → templates → push-to-talk dictation (after DPIA §3-A + §19 + measured Hinglish accuracy) → Lens shadow (after 12a; not this quarter, `ROADMAP-v2.md:428`). Ambient capture of a third party (the patient) is in no design and needs its own DPIA class analysis (R-250/R-244 family); §6 names it as not scheduled — deliberately.

- **2c is two rails at different distances.** The **lab rail is close**: `collect()` accepts a draw from any `lab.collection.operate` holder at any of five sites and binds to no chair (`modules/lab/collection.ts:308-333`; site CHECK `schema/lab.ts:316-319`). The blockers are *ordering* (the doctor advises, the lab desk orders and bills in one transaction — `plans/2026-08-28-phase1-07d-doctor-cockpit.md:133-134`; `modules/lab/desk.ts:178-182,410-420,478-489`; the placer rule needs `orders.place` + `lab.orders.place`, `kernel/orders/place.ts:294-298`, `lab/manifest.ts:104`), *payment* (doc 02 pays before `awaiting_collection`, `02-central-lab-lims.md:95-98,112` — **money**), a *location* (no room on the specimen; no chair/printer kind, `schema/resources.ts:86-88`) and a *label printer* (the FD-24 relay renders three OPD papers only, `kernel/printing/render.ts:659-661`). The **pharmacy rail is far**: the dispense is a window act ending in the pharmacist's hand-over with a second identity confirmation under the Pharmacy Act (`modules/pharmacy/handover.ts:31-35`; 16c D7 `plans/2026-09-02-phase1-16c-opd-dispense-counter.md:44`), "money moves before the drug leaves" (D8 `:45`), no delivery state or room exists (`modules/pharmacy/workflow-def.ts:33-39`), and the module is live with an empty shelf behind the un-ruled GST-on-tax-inclusive-MRP question (`wf/understand/rulings-gates.md:15`). The runner both rails want is 19a's pool engine, not this quarter (`ROADMAP-v2.md:414-415`). **Reconciled:** "eliminate the queue" = the *order* leaves the room with the doctor; the *draw* happens at the room or its ante-room by a phlebotomist paged to the floor; the *label* prints at the floor relay after the patient scan; the *drug* is verified and picked before the patient reaches the counter (doc 16:459) and handed over by a pharmacist at the room once money has moved. Two money rulings (collect-before-pay for lab; hand-over-before-settlement for pharmacy) go to the owner; the Pharmacy Act hand-over stays with the pharmacist (law, not asked).

- **Tiers, buy-not-build, DPIA, IPD gate.** Every P2 automation is T0 (predictor, breach reader) or a rule; the only model-backed rungs (Lens, scribe-to-note) are T2, gated on DPIA v0.2 + 12a + a pinned provider under DPA (`copilot-design.md:125-129`). ## 5. DECIDED and RULINGS

**DECIDED (Indian-corporate-hospital standard; one line each).**
1. Patients see **position**, staff see **minutes with an age**; the predictor is T0 and informational — L1 order is never touched (22c-D DD4; doc 20:26).
2. Pace = per-doctor median of the trailing 30 completed consults, computed on read, `n ≥ 10` else the department constant, which stays as the fallback.
3. The 20-minute rule reads the measured pace once E1-M1 lands; a doctor on leave or with an empty session never wins the comparison (the FD-7 T8 lesson).
4. Delay declaration is one tap by the doctor or PA on the session; it shifts class computation only; patients are told by template (R-020).
5. `slot_minutes_by_type` defaults to the 10-minute ruling for all types; values change only from 30 days of medians, rounded to 5, signed by the OPD head — "dynamic" = set from data, never re-ordered by a model.
6. A longer booking claims consecutive starts under the existing partial unique index; no second arbiter (22c-C DD1's rule kept).
7. `post_procedure` is a fourth visit type, not a fee class: fee follows the revisit rule and the day-care package; anchor = `daycare.discharged` with a review date, else `admissionAdvised`.
8. Overbooking is config per R-216/R-217 (policy rows): 15 % default, 25 % only when trailing no-show ≥ 12 %; unconfirmed slots released only on overbooked sessions.
9. 16b is approved as authored and re-based, not re-designed; NKDA is a positive `no_known_allergies` row with a recorder.
10. "Suggests clinical considerations" = deterministic check hits + cited restatements + protocol prompts; free-form differentials are not built (T2 cap; SaMD to counsel).
11. Rx pre-fill is department templates keyed by ICD-10 prefix, curated by the department head, applied as editable lines, checked by the same engine at issue — a "T2-equivalent (template)", never a model.
12. Every draft anywhere is evented before any model drafts: F22's `rx.warning_heeded`, the scribe's accept/discard, the template's kept/edited/removed.
13. The scribe posts the UI language; a Hindi clip is never transcribed with an English hint.
14. Ambient capture is not scheduled; the ladder is card → templates → push-to-talk → Lens shadow; ambient needs its own DPIA class analysis before it is authored.
15. The room is `collection_site='opd'` plus an additive `room_id` (registry `room`), not a sixth site value; the queue gains a room/floor filter.
16. The doctor's order door uses the existing placer rule (`orders.place` + `lab.orders.place`), items priced from the catalogue; the desk path stays for walk-ins and external prescriptions.
17. Labels print through the FD-24 relay (`lab_label` kind) at the floor printer, only after the patient scan; no printer in the room by default; `label_source` admits only `printer|downtime_kit`.
18. The phlebotomist enters only a free room; the ante-room is the default; the page is a role-broadcast alert until 19a, then a pool task.
19. Pharmacist hand-over is the terminal act, at the room or its door; assistants carry; H1 register at hand-over; X/NDPS never in a room (16c R-3).
20. No cash in consult rooms; payment before the drug moves stays (D8) unless NEW-P2-2 rules otherwise; substitution consent is captured at the seat before pick (D6).
21. Baselines accrue from UAT day one; only production data sets a target; every P2 number carries the SHA and image tag it was measured against.
22. Plan homes are letters in existing blocks (07e, 07f, 16b-ii, 17c-ii, 16c-ii, 12c); Plan 22 stays procurement; nothing is renumbered.
