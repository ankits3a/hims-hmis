# Every seat in the building — the staff census the spine must serve

Companion to `00-FABLE-REVIEW.md`. The owner's correction, 2026-09-20: *"from the main gate guard
to the ambulance driver to the receptionist to MRD ... to the owner — the approval mechanism should
be diverse and of high grade."* The review sampled staff; this doc enumerates them. Every row is a
class of person who OWES something by a time, FILES something that needs a decision, DECIDES
something, or is REACHED some way. Where a row has none of the four, it does not belong on the spine.

Columns: **owes** (obligations that fire with a clock) · **files** (requests needing a decision) ·
**decides** (their approval authority) · **chain** (who inherits their delay) · **reach** (the channel
ladder that can actually find them) · **India** (the edge case that breaks the naive design).

## Q. What the census changes in the design

The review's mechanism was written as if every recipient were a logged-in person with a smartphone
who reads English. The table above says that is roughly a third of the building. Six requirements
follow, and each is a design change, not a configuration.

1. **Eight kinds of addressee, not one.** `usersHoldingRole` resolves a role to users. The spine
   must address an obligation to a **person**, a **post** (gate, ward station, billing counter,
   OT desk, pantry), a **role**, a **unit** (the team on take), a **committee** (via its secretary),
   a **contractor** (via its supervisor), an **external party** (via the seat that owns the
   relationship), or an **agent** (via its owner). A post-addressed obligation is answered by
   whoever holds the post now, and an unstaffed post is itself a signal.

2. **Tasks and approvals are two verbs on one noun.** Support, transport, engineering and nursing
   staff receive *tasks* ("clean bed 12", "carry the sample", "fix the ICU socket"); managers
   receive *approvals*. Both are obligations with clocks, chains and records. Today the engine has
   only the approval verb.

3. **Completion evidence is per class.** An in-app decision (screen staff); a keypad press (drivers,
   night calls); a **QR scan at the location** (housekeeping, transport: the ward's QR proves
   presence); a **photo** (plumber, electrician, BMW); a read-back (critical value); a sensor value
   returning to range (fridge, gas manifold); a nurse's tap on behalf of the attendant. The
   evidence kind is declared on the obligation kind, and an obligation without its evidence is not
   closed.

4. **Reach profiles are per class, then per person.** Screen seats: station banner. Clinicians:
   app → WhatsApp → call, with roster skips. Support and contract: supervisor's phone, flash SMS
   in Hindi, missed-call acknowledgement, board. Drivers: voice only. Committees and external
   parties: email and portal. A person overrides their class; a class is the default.

5. **Language and literacy are routing inputs.** Hindi and the regional language for support
   staff; voice for anyone whose class says so; English never assumed. The templates-with-slots
   (review R-k) exist in every language the roster records for the person.

6. **Contract staff are on the spine without a login.** The contractor's roster (who holds which
   post this shift) is data the hospital must hold, and the contractor supervisor is the person
   attributed. Identity for the ack is the post phone plus the shift, not a user id.

**Sensors and machines file too.** A fridge, a gas manifold, an autoclave's failed BI, a DG that did
not start, an AEBAS device offline: each files an obligation the way a cashier files a refund. The
filer kind (person / agent / sensor / external) is on the row and on the card.

**Decided here (standard answers):** the eight addressee kinds · task and approval as two verbs on
one obligation · evidence kind declared per obligation kind · class reach profiles with per-person
override · contract staff addressed by post. **Owner rulings needed:** which support-service classes
carry obligations in phase one (all, or wards and OT first) · whether contractor supervisors are
attributed on the same ledger as employees · which languages the templates ship in first.
